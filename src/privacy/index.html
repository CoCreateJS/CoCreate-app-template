Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how CoCreate handles information collected through cocreatejs.com, its public contact forms, and website accounts or community features that link to this policy.

CoCreateJS is also self-hostable software. A person or organization operating an independent CoCreateJS deployment determines how that deployment handles information. Their privacy policy—not this one—governs information submitted to their deployment.

Information we may collect

Information you provide

Your name, email address, message content, account details, project information, contribution details, and anything else you choose to send us.

Technical information

IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, referring pages, error information, and security or diagnostic events.

Cookies and local storage

The website may use cookies, browser storage, and similar technology for sessions, preferences, security, offline behavior, and site operation.

How we use information

  • Provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the website and community features.
  • Respond to questions, contribution requests, and other communications.
  • Authenticate users, maintain sessions, and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Understand website performance and improve documentation and developer experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

How information may be shared

We may provide information to service providers that help operate hosting, communications, security, analytics, or other website functionality. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or complete a corporate transaction.

We do not describe source code, public issues, merge requests, or other intentionally public contributions as private. Review a contribution before publishing it.

Security and retention

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and maintaining public project history.

Your choices

You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser, choose what information you submit, and contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, legal obligations, or public open-source project history.

Children

The website is intended for developers, organizations, and general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website and project evolve. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to info@cocreatejs.com or CoCreate, 42 Broadway #12, New York, NY 10004, United States.
